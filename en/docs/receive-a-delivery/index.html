Receive a delivery

How to record what actually arrived against a supplier order, including a delivery that came short.

  1. Sign in and check the context at the top: store, Receiving location and Legal stock owner.
  2. Under Receiving, choose the order (Order · supplier).
  3. Under Delivery, enter the delivery document ID exactly as it is on the delivery note, plus the revision if there is one.
  4. For each line, enter Quantity received in the unit shown. Leave a line blank if nothing arrived.
  5. Check the columns Expected, Outstanding, factor and base unit, so you know what one carton is in units.
  6. Choose Review receipt. Check the base quantities and any warning, such as "More than outstanding".
  7. Choose Confirm receipt and read the result: Completed, Rejected, Held for review, Pending or Outcome unknown.
  8. Anything not delivered stays Outstanding on the order. Choose Start a new receipt for the next delivery.

If you get no answer

If you get no answer after confirming, do not enter the receipt again. See When you get no answer.
