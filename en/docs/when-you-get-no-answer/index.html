When you get no answer

If the connection drops just as you confirm a receipt, you do not know whether it was recorded. This is how you avoid recording it twice.

  1. If the result says the status could not be checked, do not enter the receipt again.
  2. Choose Send the same request again. It reuses the same operation, so the receipt cannot be recorded twice.
  3. Or choose Check status to look up the result.
  4. If the order changed after you reviewed it, choose Reload order. What you entered is kept. Review again against the new version.
  5. After signing in again, check the Operations still in doubt panel and resolve those before you confirm new receipts.
