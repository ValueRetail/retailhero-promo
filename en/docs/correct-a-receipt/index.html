Correct a receipt

How to fix a keying error without removing the original record. Correcting needs a separate permission.

  1. In Receipts and evidence, open the receipt and choose Correct this receipt.
  2. For each affected line, enter the New net quantity – what the line should total after the correction. Leave the other lines blank.
  3. Check the preview: "Change … new net …".
  4. Write the Reason for the correction in plain words.
  5. Choose Review correction, check the values and choose Confirm correction.
  6. Read the result. If it was rejected, use Revise as a new correction. If the outcome is unknown, check its status and do not enter it again.

The original receipt always stays on record. The correction is a new record that points to it.
