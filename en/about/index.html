About Retailhero

A back-office system for Norwegian grocery stores.

Retailhero is built for supermarkets with a broad range, fresh food and service counters – and for the people who receive the goods, fill the shelves and keep the figures right.

Why we are building Retailhero

We started with receiving, because that is where the stock figure begins. From there we build out: counts and waste, ordering, till reconciliation, and prices that reach the till and the shelf at the same time.

Retailhero does not replace your till or your accounts. It is the back-office system that keeps the goods and the work in the store in order, alongside the systems you already have.

Retailhero is developed by Value Retail Consulting AS in Tønsberg. The team behind it has more than 30 years of retail experience, and we build Retailhero on what we have seen work – and fail – in stockrooms and stores.
