Receiving

When a delivery arrives short

The order said twelve cartons of milk. Nine arrived. You record what actually came, line by line, and the rest stays on the order as outstanding.

Discrepancies, damaged cartons and shortages are followed up with the supplier before the rest is confirmed. If the connection drops and you send twice, the delivery is still received only once.

Stock explanation

From opening to closing, line by line

Retailhero shows how every product went from opening to closing stock: received, sold, waste and corrections.

Typed 12 instead of 6? Fix it with a correction and a reason. The original receipt stays on record, so the store manager sees the whole story.

Counts and waste

Waste at closing time

Bread that reached its date today goes in as waste, with a reason. A count records the difference from what the system expected instead of overwriting the figure.

The next morning you can see what happened to the item – and over time, where waste really comes from.

Prices and shelf labels

The new price on the shelf before opening

A new price is approved by someone other than the person who entered it, and goes to the till and the electronic shelf labels at the same time.

A label counts as updated only when the label itself has confirmed it – not when someone pressed a button. You see which labels are lagging before a customer does.

How it is built

The stock figure can be explained, line by line.

Every stock change is stored as a new record with who, when and why. Old records are never edited or deleted.

A correction is a new record
It points to the old one, which stays. The database rejects updates and deletes on stock movements.
Exact quantities
Whole numbers in an approved unit, with the conversion to base units shown before you confirm. No rounding.
Never counted twice
Send the same receipt again and it is the same operation. When two receipts race for the same quantity, exactly one wins.
The store keeps going
The screens say when figures are not up to date, and the store is built to keep working when the connection drops.
